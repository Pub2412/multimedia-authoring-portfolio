Thriller

The sixth studio album by Michael Jackson, released on November 30, 1982. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest.

Billie Jean

The song was inspired by several experiences rather than one specific person. Michael Jackson said that "Billie Jean" represented the groupies who followed him and his brothers during their time with The Jackson 5. Some women falsely claimed that members of the group had fathered their children, creating emotional and legal concerns. Michael also revealed that he received disturbing letters from a woman who insisted he was the father of her twins. The harassment became so intense that she even sent him a gun and urged him to take his own life after she and her child did. While this incident influenced his perspective, Michael maintained that the character "Billie Jean" was fictional, created as a symbol of obsessive fans and false accusations rather than based on a single individual.
